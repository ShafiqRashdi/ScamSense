menyemak sama ada pautan atau mesej mungkin penipuan. Borang ini tidak meminta nama, e-mel atau

*ScamSense test. Thank you for helping. ScamSense is a free website that checks whether a link or
message may be a scam. This form does not ask for your name, email or phone number. Do not paste
real messages containing your personal details.*

## [For you] Expected answers and use in Chapter 5
- Q3 → Likely a scam; Q4 → No warning signs. These are the two acceptance criteria, tested with real users.
- Q5 and Q6 → second option. Q5 is the key finding: whether "No warning signs" is misread as "safe".
- Q1–Q2 let you split results by age and smartphone confidence.
